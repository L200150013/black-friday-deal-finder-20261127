# Price Proof — Black Friday deals, checked

A static Black Friday / Cyber Monday deal finder. 34 real deals across 6 stores
(Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, Target, Kohl's, Wayfair), each checked on Black
Friday itself, 27 November 2026, at the store or in a deals source that names
the store — with sale price, usual price, discount, store link, check time,
source, and an honest verdict on every deal:

- **Record Low** — the lowest price the source has tracked
- **Strong Deal** — a genuinely good price, well under the usual price
- **Usual Price** — the cut is real, but ordinary

Search and filters by category, store, discount and verdict all work, with
sorting by biggest saving, biggest discount, lowest or highest price. Same
product at two stores is shown side by side where it matters (AirPods Pro 3:
Amazon $149 vs Walmart $179; Galaxy S26 Ultra: Best Buy $950 vs Amazon
$1,049; MacBook Air M5: Best Buy $999 vs Amazon $1,049).

Static files under `app/` — no build step, no network request, no tracking.
Open `app/index.html` in desktop Chrome or on a phone.
